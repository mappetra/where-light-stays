Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Info: We collect your email or username used for authentication via Firebase.
  • User Content: The memories (text and links) you choose to post on the map.
  • Location Data: Coordinates you explicitly provide when pinning a memory.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly to operate the map, allow you to save your memories, and enable other users to see them if you choose to make them public.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We use Firebase (Google) as a secure infrastructure to host your account and data, which operates under its own security standards.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your account and all associated memories at any time.
  • Request the removal of specific memories.

5. Security

We take standard measures to protect your data. However, please remember that memories posted publicly on the map are visible to other users. You are responsible for the information you choose to share.

6. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact the site administrator directly.
